Our Story

Agriscan started in the year 2025 as a simple crop disease diagnosis tool. At that time, only two crops could be scanned: potato and maize. Over the months, it has revolutionized to become a multi-crop diseases detection tool with AI support and market connectivity, transforming how farmers manage their Agricultural processes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to contribute to a more sustainable agricultural world by solving critical problems: late crop disease detection, lack of reliable specialized agricultural advice, and fragmented market connectivity. We prevent yield loss and crop rot, ensuring food security and stable income for farmers.

Value Proposition

The platform allows farmers to upload images of crop leaves, which are analyzed using trained deep learning models which detects and classifies crop diseases. Once a disease is identified, the farmer can seek treatment recommendations and preventive guidance from the integrated Knowledge-Based AI assistant helping farmers respond quickly and effectively. The AI agricultural assistant capable of providing contextual farming advice in both English and Kiswahili. The assistant supports farmers in decision-making related to crop management, pest control, agricultural best practices and integrates location specific weather API to provide weather-based advice. AgriScan also integrates a role-based agricultural marketplace that connects farmers, produce buyers, and agrovet agencies. Farmers can discover potential buyers and seedling companies in their locality, buyers can source produce directly from farmers, and agrovet agencies can list services and products to support farm operations.
