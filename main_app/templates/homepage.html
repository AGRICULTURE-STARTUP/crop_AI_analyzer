Our Story

Agriscan started in the year 2025 as a simple crop disease diagnosis tool. At that time, only two crops could be scanned: potato and maize. Over the years, it has revolutionized to become a multi-crop diseases detection tool with AI support and market connectivity, transforming how farmers manage their fields.

Our Mission

Our mission is to contribute to a more sustainable agricultural world by solving critical problems: late crop disease detection, lack of reliable specialized agricultural advice, and fragmented market connectivity. We prevent yield loss and crop rot, ensuring food security and stable income for farmers.

Value Proposition

The novelty of AgriScan lies in its integration of three critical agricultural support systems: AI-based crop disease diagnosis, multilingual AI advisory services, and a digital agricultural marketplace—within a single scalable platform.

Unlike many agricultural tools that focus on a single problem, AgriScan addresses diagnosis, knowledge access, and market connectivity simultaneously, creating a comprehensive digital ecosystem designed specifically for smallholder farmers.
